I am a Bioinformatics Programmer in the [Evrony Lab](https://www.evronylab.org/) at [NYU Langone Health](https://med.nyu.edu/centers-programs/human-genetics-genomics/). Previously, I worked as a Computational Biology and Data Science Co-op at [NextRNA Therapeutics](http://nextrna.com/) contributing to RNA-directed medicine workflows.

I hold an M.S. in Bioinformatics from [Northeastern University](https://cos.northeastern.edu/master-of-science-in-bioinformatics/), with research experience spanning computational chemistry, sequence analysis, and biological data engineering.

## Professional interests

- Reproducible bioinformatics pipeline development
- Computational drug discovery and structural bioinformatics
- Machine learning for biological data interpretation
