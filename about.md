## Preface

I am a Bioinformatics Programmer in the [Evrony Lab](https://www.evronylab.org/) at [NYU Langone Health](https://med.nyu.edu/centers-programs/human-genetics-genomics/). I previously worked as a Computational Biology and Data Science Co-op at [NextRNA Therapeutics](http://nextrna.com/), where I contributed to RNA-directed medicine development.

My academic training includes an M.S. in Bioinformatics from [Northeastern University](https://cos.northeastern.edu/master-of-science-in-bioinformatics/), with prior research experience in computational chemistry and enzyme/cofactor analysis workflows.

## Focus

I am motivated by research problems at the intersection of biology and computation, especially:

- Bioinformatics software and reproducible pipelines
- Computational drug discovery and structural bioinformatics
- Machine learning for biological data interpretation

## Interests

Outside work, I enjoy digital illustration, reading, hiking, and learning new technical skills.
