## Preface
It is possible that my fascination for science began from my first glance through the microscope at Paramecium back in the biology lab at school. Since then I have shown a predilection to learn about the world imperceptible by human eyes. As I got older I was intrigued by the interrelation of biology with computation, mathematics and technology, which led me to be inspired by the vast field of bioinformatics that perfectly amalgamates two of my most passionate fields - computer science and biology. My aim is fueled by a conviction to make a dent, if not a difference, in the field of medical drug designing, machine learning in bioinformatics, stem cell and cancer therapeutics.

## Certification courses
**[Sequence Models, October 2025](https://www.coursera.org/learn/nlp-sequence-models/home/info)**<br>
I learned the core concepts behind sequence models and how they are applied to problems involving sequential data such as natural language and speech. I built and trained Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and explored commonly used variants including Long Short-Term Memory networks (LSTMs) and Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs). Through practical exercises, I applied these models to tasks such as character-level language modeling and text generation, gaining a deeper understanding of how neural networks process sequences and capture contextual information over time. I also gained experience with natural language processing techniques, including the use of word embeddings to represent semantic relationships between words. In addition, I worked with modern NLP tools such as HuggingFace tokenizers and transformer-based models to perform tasks like named entity recognition and question answering. Overall, this course strengthened my understanding of sequence modeling and modern NLP methods, and gave me practical experience applying deep learning techniques to real-world language processing problems.

**[Neural Networks and Deep Learning, October 2025](https://www.coursera.org/learn/neural-networks-deep-learning/home/info)**<br> 
I learned the fundamental concepts underlying neural networks and deep learning, including how to design, build, and train fully connected deep neural networks. Through hands-on implementation, I developed an understanding of how neural networks learn from data, how to structure network architectures, and how key parameters such as weights, biases, activation functions, and hyperparameters influence model performance. I also learned to implement neural networks efficiently using vectorized computations, which improves computational performance and scalability. In addition to the technical implementation, the course helped me understand the broader technological trends driving the rapid advancement of deep learning and its growing impact across fields such as computer vision, natural language processing, and data science. This course provided me with a strong foundation for understanding modern AI systems and applying deep learning methods to real-world problems.

By the end of the course, I had a solid understanding of the applications of WGS in surveillance of bacteria, including species identification, typing, and characterization of antimicrobial resistance and virulence traits, as well as plasmid characterization. **[Genomic Data Science: Offered by Johns Hopkins University on Coursera, Nov 2019](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/genomic-data-science)**<br>
Through this course, I was able to gain insights about how genomics is revolutionizing medical discoveries and that it's neccessity in understanding the genome and leverage the data and information from genomic datasets. The course covered the concepts and tools to understand, analyze, and interpret data from next-generation sequencing experiments. Through this course, I learned about the most common tools used in genomic data science, including the use of the command line, along with a variety of software implementation tools like Python, R, and Bioconductor. The course was designed to serve as both a standalone introduction to genomic data science or as a perfect complement to a primary degree or postdoc in biology, molecular biology, or genetics. As a scientist in these fields, seeking to gain familiarity in data science and statistical tools to better interact with the data in my everyday work, I found this course to be highly valuable.
